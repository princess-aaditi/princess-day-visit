The Princess Day Visit
👑

Aaditi, someone is at your door

It’s Princess Day, you’re unwell, and Shree could not stay away.

He is standing outside with his hands full. Stay in bed. Take your time. This visit is all yours.

Unlock the stars

He has put a star lock on your door, and only you can open it. Follow the glowing star, one after another.

He brought you a crown

The first thing he does is open a small velvet box. Three tiaras, because he could not choose. You can.

Knight Shree vs the germs

Then he looks around your room and sees what has been troubling his princess. Tap each germ and he will chase it out.

The royal healing spell

He rolls up his sleeves and unpacks his bag. Tap everything he brings to your bedside.

Calm your knight

He has done everything he can, and he is still worried sick. Only your promises can fix that.

Catch his kisses

You smiled, so now he is showing off. He steps back and starts blowing kisses across the room. Catch ten.

How much does he love you?

He sits on the edge of your bed and asks you to guess.

Letters from his pocket

He pulls out six folded notes. He wrote them on the way here and almost lost his nerve.

Tap an envelope.

Light the candles

He turns the lights down and sets five candles around your bed. Light each one.

Feel my heartbeat

He sits down beside you and puts your hand on his chest. Tap along.

Things I never say

With your hand still on his heart, he finally says what he usually keeps in. Touch each one to read it.

Stay in my arms

He stops talking and simply holds you. Press and hold. Stay as long as you can.

Seven wishes

He opens your window. He brought seven lanterns, with one wish for you folded inside each. Tap a lantern to let it fly.

He takes both your hands. “Princess Day ends tonight. I don’t want it to.”

Will you be my princess on every day after this one?

Your royal orders

👑

Royal Proclamation

On this Princess Day, Aaditi is crowned

By her command, Shree shall deliver:

    My Aaditi,

    I could not really knock on your door today, so I built this visit. Every candle, every kiss and every lantern in it is something I ache to do in person.

    You are the best thing that has ever happened to me. You are the calm I look for, the face I search every room for, and the one I want beside me in every version of my life.

    I hate that you’re unwell and I’m far away. Heal slowly and properly, my love. Let me do the worrying until your sparkle is back.

    I love you.

    Your knight, always. Shree
